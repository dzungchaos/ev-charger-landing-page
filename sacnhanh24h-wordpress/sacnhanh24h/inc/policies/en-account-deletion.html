This page explains how to request deletion of your Account and personal data in the Sạc Nhanh 24H app (name shown on the App Store and Google Play), developed and operated by Công ty Cổ phần Trạm Sạc Nhanh 24H ("Tram Sac Nhanh 24H", "we" or "us").

You can submit a request directly in the App (Option 1). If you no longer have the App installed or cannot sign in, send your request by email or through the hotline (Option 2).

Option 1: Delete your Account in the App

  1. Open the Sạc Nhanh 24H app and sign in to the Account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Tài khoản (Account) → Quyền riêng tư (Privacy) → Xoá tài khoản (Delete account).
  3. Choose a reason (optional), then type the phrase XOA TAI KHOAN exactly to confirm.
  4. Submit. The Account changes to "pending deletion" and all sign-in sessions on all devices are revoked.

Option 2: Request by email or hotline

  • Email: write to sacnhanh24h@gmail.com with the subject "Yêu cầu xoá dữ liệu cá nhân – [registered phone number]" (Personal data deletion request). Please send it from the email address registered to the Account. If you registered with a phone number, state that phone number in the email.
  • Hotline: +84 921 688 788 (8:00–17:30, Monday to Friday, except public holidays).
  • In your request, state whether you want to delete the whole Account or only part of your data.
  • To protect you, we verify that the sender is the Account holder (for example by sending an OTP to the registered phone number or email) before processing. We never ask for your password.

Conditions for deleting an Account

  • No charging session is in progress.
  • No Wallet top-up is pending.
  • The Wallet balance (both the main balance and the promotional balance) is zero. If the Wallet still has a balance, the system does not allow deletion yet; please contact Customer Care. After verification, the unused main balance is refunded to the bank account or e-wallet used for the top-up (or to a bank account in your name if a refund to the source is not possible) within 7 working days, free of charge; the promotional balance is cancelled. You can then continue deleting the Account.
  • If you have a reservation at a charging station, it is cancelled when you confirm deletion.

What happens after you submit a request

  • Immediately: the Account changes to "pending deletion" and sign-in sessions are revoked; the Account can no longer top up or make reservations.
  • During the next 30 days: if you change your mind, contact Customer Care to cancel the deletion request.
  • After 30 days from a valid request: the system automatically deletes or anonymises data as shown in the table below. After this step, the Account cannot be restored.

Deleting part of your data without deleting the Account

  • In the App: you can edit or delete Vehicle information, edit your profile, unlink Google/Apple/Facebook (OTP verification required; the last sign-in method cannot be unlinked if the Account has no password), and turn off promotional notifications.
  • Other data: send a request by email or hotline as in Option 2, stating which data to delete. We confirm receipt within 2 working days and complete the request within 20 days.

If you signed in with Facebook

  • When you sign in with Facebook, the App receives only your Facebook identifier, name and email. This data is deleted when you delete your Account using Option 1 or Option 2, or when you unlink Facebook in the App.
  • You can also remove the App's access directly on Facebook: go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites, select Sạc Nhanh 24H and tap Remove.
  • For Google and Apple, you can remove access in the same way in the third-party app management section of your Google account or Apple ID.
