This Policy explains how Công ty Cổ phần Trạm Sạc Nhanh 24H ("Tram Sac Nhanh 24H", "we" or "us") collects, uses, shares, stores and protects the personal data of users ("Users", "you") when you use the Trạm Sạc Nhanh 24H mobile application (the "App"), our charging station network (the "Charging Stations") and the website sacnhanh24h.vn. This Policy is prepared under the Law on Personal Data Protection No. 91/2025/QH15, Decree No. 356/2025/ND-CP and other relevant regulations of Vietnam.

Tram Sac Nhanh 24H is the party that decides the purposes and means of processing the personal data described in this Policy.

1. Personal data we collect

We do not collect or store your bank card number, CVV code, or e-wallet password or PIN; this information is entered and processed directly by the payment gateway. We do not require your citizen ID, date of birth or gender to use the Services.

Location data may be classified as sensitive personal data under the law. You can refuse or revoke location access in your device settings; you can still find stations by name or area.

2. Purposes of processing

  • Creating and managing the Account, verifying by OTP, signing in with Google, Apple, Facebook.
  • Finding the nearest charging station, reserving, starting and monitoring charging sessions, calculating charges and idle fees.
  • Topping up the Wallet, deducting payments, reconciling with payment gateways, refunds.
  • Issuing electronic invoices at your request.
  • Providing support and handling complaints.
  • Sending notifications about charging sessions, transactions and changes to the Terms; sending promotional information unless you turn this notification group off.
  • Security: detecting and preventing unauthorised access and fraud; temporarily locking sign-in after repeated incorrect passwords.
  • Statistics and improving the quality of the Services and the charging network.
  • Complying with legal obligations (accounting, tax, invoicing, requests from competent state authorities).

3. Your consent

  • When registering an Account, or when signing in with Google, Apple, Facebook for the first time, you tick a box to agree to the Terms of Use and this Policy. The box is not pre-ticked. We record the version of the documents you agreed to; when they change, the App asks you to agree again.
  • You can turn promotional notifications on or off at any time in the App's notification settings.
  • You have the right to withdraw consent. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before it. For data required to provide the Services (for example the phone number or email used to sign in), withdrawing consent means we can no longer provide the Services and will handle it as a request to delete the Account.
  • In some cases permitted by law, we may process data without consent (for example to meet accounting and tax obligations or requests from competent state authorities).
  • The Services are intended for persons with full civil legal capacity. We do not knowingly collect data of children.

6. Data protection

  • Connections between the App, the website and our servers are encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Passwords are one-way encrypted (BCrypt); OTP codes and session tokens are stored only as hashes; Apple access tokens are stored encrypted.
  • Sign-in to the Account is temporarily locked after several consecutive incorrect passwords.
  • Staff access data only according to their assigned roles; administrative actions are recorded in audit logs. Passwords, OTP codes and authentication tokens are masked in logs.
  • Charging station partners cannot see your identifying information.
  • In the event of a personal data breach or loss, we notify the competent authority and affected Users within the time limits required by law.
  • No system is absolutely secure. Please protect your device, password and OTP.
