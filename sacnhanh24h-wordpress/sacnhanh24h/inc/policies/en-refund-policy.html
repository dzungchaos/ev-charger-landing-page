This Policy sets out how Công ty Cổ phần Trạm Sạc Nhanh 24H ("Tram Sac Nhanh 24H", "we" or "us") delivers the charging service, suspends or terminates it, and handles refunds for users ("Users") of the Trạm Sạc Nhanh 24H app (the "App") and our charging stations. This Policy forms an integral part of the Terms of Use. Complaints are received and resolved under the complaint handling procedure (Vietnamese).

1. How the online charging service is delivered

The Tram Sac Nhanh 24H electric vehicle charging service is delivered online through the Trạm Sạc Nhanh 24H app (iOS/Android), which connects directly to our chargers, in 4 steps:

  1. Step 1 – Find and choose a charger: open the App, choose a station on the map and check that a connector is free.
  2. Step 2 – Connect and start the session: park in the correct bay and plug the connector firmly into your vehicle. In the App, scan the QR code on the charger (or choose the connector code in the App) and tap "Start charging".
  3. Step 3 – Monitor the session: the system communicates with the vehicle and delivers energy; you can follow the battery percentage, charging power (kW) and estimated cost in real time in the App.
  4. Step 4 – Finish and pay: when the desired battery level is reached or you tap "Stop charging" in the App, the charger cuts the power and unlocks the connector. Unplug the connector and hang it back on its holder; the system automatically closes the bill and takes payment.

2. Suspension and termination of the service

  • Termination by the User: you may stop using the service and ask to close or delete your account at any time after paying all charges incurred (if any). See Account and Data Deletion.
  • Suspension or termination by Tram Sac Nhanh 24H: we may suspend an account or stop providing the service if the User seriously breaches fire safety rules at a station, tampers with equipment without authorisation, or has overdue charging fees after several reminders.

3. Cases refunded in full (the overcharged or erroneous amount)

  • You were charged successfully but the charger delivered no energy because of a technical fault on the Tram Sac Nhanh 24H side.
  • The system charged twice for the same session, or applied a unit price different from the publicly listed price at the time of charging.
  • You topped up a wrong amount into your prepaid account, or you ask to withdraw your unused top-up balance when permanently closing your account (after all charging sessions have been reconciled).

4. Cases not refunded

  • The session delivered the full amount of energy (kWh) to the vehicle battery as shown.
  • The session was interrupted by a fault in the vehicle's charging port or battery, or because you pressed the emergency stop button on the charger.
  • A valid idle fee charged because the vehicle was not moved more than 10 minutes after charging was complete.

5. Refund method and time

Refunds are returned to the same bank account, payment card or e-wallet you used for the transaction, within 07 to 15 working days (depending on the processing cycle of the card-issuing bank or e-wallet).
