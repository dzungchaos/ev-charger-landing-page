This Policy sets out how Công ty Cổ phần Trạm Sạc Nhanh 24H ("Tram Sac Nhanh 24H", "we" or "us") receives and resolves complaints and handles refunds for users ("Users") of the Sạc Nhanh 24H app (the "App") and our charging stations. This Policy forms an integral part of the Terms of Use.

1. Support and complaint channels

When you encounter a technical problem, have questions about charges or service quality, or need help, contact our Customer Care team through:

  • In the App: Support → create a ticket, choose the type (charger fault, payment, account, feedback, other), link it to the related station or charging session and attach up to 5 photos (JPEG, PNG or WEBP, up to 5 MB each).
  • Hotline: +84 921 688 788 (8:00–17:30, Monday to Friday, except public holidays).
  • Email: sacnhanh24h@gmail.com
  • Address: 5th Floor, IC Building, 82 Duy Tan Street, Cau Giay Ward, Hanoi, Vietnam.

To speed things up, please provide the charging session code (printed on the receipt in the App) or the top-up transaction code, the time, the station name, a description of the problem and photos (if any).

2. Complaint handling process and time limits

  • Time limit to file a complaint: within thirty (30) days from the date the problem occurred or the date the User discovered a billing error.
  • Initial response: within 24 working hours of receipt.
  • Resolution: Customer Care checks the charging session data, charger logs and transactions, then sends a proposed resolution through the App or by email within 03 to 05 working days.
  • Complex cases: when the problem involves hardware infrastructure, telecommunications operators, payment gateways or intermediary banks, the time limit may be extended but by no more than 14 working days; we will inform the User of the progress and the reason for the extension.
  • After resolution: a resolved support ticket can be reopened within 30 days; tickets with no further reply are closed automatically after 7 days.
  • If you do not agree with the outcome, you have the right to ask the state authority for consumer protection for assistance, or to choose negotiation, mediation, arbitration or the courts in accordance with the law.

3. Cases eligible for a refund

  • The User was charged twice, or charged differently from the price published at the start of the charging session, because of a software or transaction error.
  • A charging session had a problem caused by a charger or system of Tram Sac Nhanh 24H (for example energy recorded incorrectly), so the amount deducted does not match the energy actually charged.
  • An idle fee was charged in breach of the Terms of Use (for example while the charger reported a fault, during a power outage or emergency stop).
  • Other cases reviewed and approved by Tram Sac Nhanh 24H as a goodwill gesture.

Where money was debited at the payment gateway but the Wallet was not credited: we reconcile automatically with the payment gateway; if it is confirmed that the gateway collected the money, the amount is credited to the Wallet. The result is communicated within 03 working days.

4. Cases not eligible for a refund

  • Energy actually charged into the Vehicle at the published price, including when the User stops the session early.
  • Service interruptions caused by telecommunications operators, national grid incidents, weather, natural disasters or force majeure; in these cases the User only pays for the energy actually charged.
  • Amounts paid with the promotional balance or vouchers: these cannot be exchanged for cash and are not refunded.
  • Charges arising from the User's breach of the Terms of Use (for example fraud or unauthorised interference with chargers).

5. Refund method and timing

  • Refund requests are created by Customer Care after verifying the complaint; refunds of large amounts require an additional level of approval.
  • The refund is credited to the main balance of the Wallet in the App as soon as the request is approved; the User can see it in the Wallet transaction history.
  • The maximum refund equals the real money the User paid for that transaction (excluding amounts paid with promotions or vouchers). Reward points granted for the transaction may be withdrawn when the transaction is refunded in full.
  • Refunds to the bank account or e-wallet used for the top-up apply only to the unused main balance when the User stops using the Services or deletes the Account (see section 6).
  • If a refunded charging session already has a VAT invoice, we handle the adjusting or replacement invoice in accordance with invoicing regulations. Refunded sessions cannot be invoiced from the App; please contact Customer Care.

6. Wallet balance

  • The Wallet balance can only be used to pay for services in the App; it cannot be withdrawn as cash or transferred to another person.
  • The promotional balance cannot be exchanged for cash, refunded or transferred.
  • If the User stops using the Services or wants to delete the Account while the Wallet still has a balance: the User contacts Customer Care; after verification, the unused main balance is refunded to the bank account or e-wallet used for the top-up (or to a bank account in the User's name if a refund to the source is not possible) within 7 working days, free of charge; the promotional balance is cancelled. See the Account and Data Deletion guide.
