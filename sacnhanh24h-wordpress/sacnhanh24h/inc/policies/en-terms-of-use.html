6. Prices, Wallet and payment

6.1. Charging price is calculated as energy (kWh) multiplied by the unit price for the time slot and day applicable at each Charging Station. The unit price is shown in the App before charging and is fixed at the moment the charging session starts.

6.2. Top-up: the User tops up the Wallet through VNPay, MoMo or ZaloPay. The limit per top-up and the maximum Wallet balance are shown in the App (at the time of publication: from VND 10,000 to VND 10,000,000 per top-up; maximum balance VND 10,000,000). Tram Sac Nhanh 24H currently charges no top-up fee; if a surcharge is introduced, it will be clearly shown before the User confirms.

6.3. The Wallet is credited only when the payment gateway confirms the transaction as successful. If money has been debited but the Wallet has not been credited, the Refund and Complaints Policy applies.

6.4. Wallet balance consists of the main balance (money topped up by the User) and the promotional balance (given by Tram Sac Nhanh 24H). When paying, the promotional balance is used first, then the main balance. The Wallet balance can only be used to pay for the Services; it cannot be withdrawn as cash or transferred to another person. The promotional balance cannot be exchanged for cash.

6.5. Starting a session: the Wallet must hold at least the minimum balance shown for each Charging Station (VND 50,000 by default).

6.6. Paying for a session: the amount is deducted from the Wallet when the charging session ends; nothing is held in advance. The session stops automatically when the balance is about to run out. Because of data transmission delays from the charger, the balance may become negative by a small amount; this negative amount is offset against the next top-up.

6.7. Incidents during a session: in the event of a power outage or loss of charger connection, the User pays only for the energy actually delivered by the charger. If the charger cannot send the final reading, the session is settled based on data recorded by the charger; the User may file a complaint under the Refund and Complaints Policy.

6.8. Vouchers and promotions apply under the conditions of each programme; the validity of a voucher is checked at the moment the charging session starts; vouchers cannot be exchanged for cash.

6.9. Invoices: the receipt in the App is not a VAT invoice. The User may request an electronic invoice in the App for charging sessions within the same month, in the current or the previous month, by entering the buyer's details (individual or business with a tax code).

7. Idle fee

7.1. When the Vehicle has stopped drawing power (fully charged or the charging limit set by the Vehicle or the User has been reached) while the connector is still plugged in, the User has a free grace period to unplug and move the Vehicle. The default grace period is ten (10) minutes and may differ between Charging Stations; the applicable period is shown in the App.

7.2. After the grace period, an idle fee is charged per minute until the connector is unplugged from the Vehicle. The per-minute fee and the maximum cap per session are published in the App and/or at the Charging Station.

7.3. No idle fee is charged when the charger reports a fault, during a power outage or emergency stop, or when operating staff stop the charging session.

7.4. The idle fee is deducted automatically from the Wallet and shown on the session receipt; the idle fee never makes the Wallet balance negative.
